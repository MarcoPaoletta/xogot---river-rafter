Tilt Landscape: 5 Game Concepts

Every concept is a 3D game played in landscape and steered by tilting the iPhone. The concept art is drawn in code (SVG).

Open this page in Safari and tap the button to make the hero in each picture follow your phone's tilt. The Files preview doesn't run scripts, so there you only see the static pictures.

Physics puzzle-platformer · Easiest to build

1. Marble Valley

Tilt the world to roll a glass marble across floating grassy islands, narrow bridges, and ramps. Collect gems, don't fall off, and reach the goal flag before time runs out.

Tilt controls

  • Tilt sets the gravity direction, so the marble rolls downhill
  • Optional: the level tilts a little for visual feedback
  • Tap to jump (optional)

Game loop

  • Short levels (30–90 s)
  • Gems unlock new islands
  • Star rating by time

Free asset packs (CC0)

  • Quaternius: Stylized Nature MegaKit
  • Quaternius: Ultimate Nature Pack
  • Poly Haven: sky HDRI and rock/wood textures
Godot: RigidBody3D sphere, CSG or GridMap islands, Area3D gems, follow camera. Difficulty: ★☆☆☆☆
Endless flyer · Arcade

2. Canyon Runner

Pilot a small ship low through a desert canyon at sunset. Bank around rock pillars, fly through rings for a boost, and survive as long as you can while the speed keeps rising.

Tilt controls

  • Tilt left/right to bank and steer
  • Tilt forward/back to dive or climb
  • Hold a finger on the screen to boost

Game loop

  • Endless run, scored by distance and rings
  • Unlock ship skins
  • Daily seeded canyon

Free asset packs (CC0)

  • Quaternius: Ultimate Spaceships Pack
  • Quaternius: Stylized Nature MegaKit (rocks/cliffs)
  • ambientCG / Poly Haven: sandstone textures, sunset HDRI
Godot: recycled canyon chunks (object pooling), kinematic ship, Area3D rings. Difficulty: ★★☆☆☆
Downhill river ride · Relaxed to intense

3. River Rafter

Steer a log raft down a winding forest river. Dodge rocks, ride the rapids, and pick up floating supplies. Calm stretches give way to waterfalls and white water.

Tilt controls

  • Tilt left/right to steer across the current
  • Tilt back to slow down and brace
  • The current always pushes you forward

Game loop

  • Hand-built river routes
  • Supplies upgrade the raft
  • Docks act as checkpoints

Free asset packs (CC0)

  • Quaternius: Ultimate Nature Pack (pines, rocks, logs)
  • KayKit: Forest Nature Pack
  • Poly Haven / ambientCG: water normal maps, grass, riverbed
Godot: Path3D river with a flow direction, simple water shader, raft that moves relative to the path. Difficulty: ★★★☆☆
Herding sim · Cozy

4. Sheepdog Hills

You're a sheepdog on rolling green hills. Run close to the sheep to push them, keep the flock together, and guide them into the pen before sunset. Watch out for wolves at dusk.

Tilt controls

  • Tilt to run in any direction
  • The harder you tilt, the faster the dog runs
  • Tap to bark, which scatters the sheep or speeds them up

Game loop

  • Each level has a flock size and a time limit
  • New farms and terrain types
  • Stars for speed and for sheep you didn't lose

Free asset packs (CC0)

  • Quaternius: Ultimate Animated Animal Pack (check that it has a dog and a sheep)
  • Quaternius: Stylized Nature MegaKit
  • Poly Haven: meadow HDRI
Godot: CharacterBody3D dog, simple flocking AI for the sheep, heightmap terrain. Difficulty: ★★★★☆
Atmospheric explorer · Beautiful

5. Wisp of the Woods

A glowing forest spirit floats through a dark woodland at night. Touch old stone lanterns to relight them and push back the darkness, and follow firefly trails to hidden groves.

Tilt controls

  • Tilt to float in any direction
  • The wisp drifts with momentum, so it feels floaty
  • Lit lanterns become checkpoints

Game loop

  • Relight every lantern in a region
  • The world changes as it gets brighter
  • Collectible spirit seeds

Free asset packs (CC0)

  • KayKit: Forest Nature Pack
  • Quaternius: Ultimate Nature Pack + Fantasy Props MegaKit (lanterns)
  • Poly Haven: night HDRI, moss textures
Godot: OmniLight3D per lantern, GPUParticles3D fireflies, volumetric fog and glow. Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Asset sources (no Kenney)

  • quaternius.com: low-poly packs, CC0 (free, including commercial use, no credit needed)
  • kaylousberg.itch.io (KayKit): stylized packs, the free editions are CC0
  • polyhaven.com: HDRI skies, textures, models, CC0
  • ambientcg.com: PBR textures, CC0

Confirm the license on each download page. The packs come as glTF/GLB or FBX files, and Godot 4 imports glTF directly.

How tilt will work in Godot

  • Read Input.get_gravity() each physics frame
  • Lock the project to landscape and remap the X and Y axes, because the sensor reports values relative to portrait
  • Calibrate at start, so the player can hold the phone at a comfortable angle
  • Add a dead zone and smoothing, plus a keyboard fallback for testing in the editor
